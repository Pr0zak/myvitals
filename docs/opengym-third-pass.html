The headline

openGym treats model output as untrusted input and validates it in code against the real library before it reaches a card. myvitals applies that discipline only where a medical number forced it. api/coach/core/validate.js is explicit that the boundary is the validator and not the prompt: a hostile note in free text can talk the model into saying anything at all, but it cannot invent a change type. Every proposed change is resolved against the real exercise library, refused when it names something not in the plan, and matched against a closed list with no default case.

myvitals already knows this pattern — assess_meal_fat re-judges every AI meal suggestion and discards the model's own verdict; fasting_coach overrides break_now for a religious fast. But strength_nudge takes block.input verbatim, JSON-dumps it, caches it in ai_summaries, and both clients render the raw slug as prose. A hallucinated exercise id becomes a plausible name with a confident reason and only fails on tap; a valid-but-wrong-muscle swap never fails at all.

Where this sits

Two openGym passes preceded this one. #ENHANCE (researched July 2026, shipped v0.7.322→342) read the project up to ~v1.2.5. #OG2 (August 2026) re-read everything since and ran phases A through D out to v0.37.0, refusing OG2-C1, D6's headline, D8, D10, D12, D13 and D16 on measurement. Twenty-four items were confirmed already shipped in that pass alone.

So the bar here is high and the yield is correspondingly narrow: 49 findings, 31 survived the first check, and roughly two thirds of those did not survive the refuter. The refusals are recorded in full below, because a measured refusal is what stops an idea returning next quarter — and several of them are refusals against this user's own production data rather than against taste.

Batch D — integrations that fail loudly

11
Classify an integration failure and stop retrying the class that cannot recover
OG3-D1livevalue 3Mbackendin progress

openGym reduces every job failure to a named class before anything acts on it — timeout, missing, auth, provider, unusable, internal — and lets the class drive behaviour: an answer that failed validation and was paid for is never retried; a call that never reached the model is. myvitals has this taxonomy inside the LLM layer only.

The evidence is live rather than hypothetical, and it is the box at the top of this page. TODO.md predicted this failure in prose under GH-EXPIRY; the database shows it happening for the second time. A four-value enum across four integrations is the right size — do not build a pipeline.

Touches an IntegrationError with kind ∈ {transient, auth, config, upstream} in integrations/, raised by google_health.valid_access_token (map invalid_grant → auth), strava_web.CookieExpired and concept2. Persist last_error_kind; tasks/scheduled.py skips auth polls until a reconnect clears it; analytics/data_health.py surfaces auth as a reconnect action, not staleness — which generalises the one-off Strava banner from v0.7.319.

Declined, with reasons

Refuted on this user's own data

Refuse an e1RM above 12 reps instead of clamping
Of 876 logged sets, the number with weight > 0 and reps > 12 is zero. The clamp has never bound and cannot under goal=hypertrophy. A clamp at 12 is a conservative floor; uncapped Epley is the fantasy the cap prevents. Returning None would also sort a real lift to the bottom of /records via two or 0.0 call sites — a different lie about a number that has never been wrong.
Count MEV/MAV volume in hard sets
Last 28 days: 129 sets, 2 rated ≤ 3. Full history 128 of 876, and 124 of those land in two weeks in late May. On a hard-set basis all 14 muscles would read "under" every week forever — the HEALTH-1 failure with the sign flipped. Correct in the literature, inapplicable to a user who does not train near failure.
Plot weekly effort beside weekly volume · Report the effort distribution
Weekly average rating over 16 weeks sits inside 4.08–4.60 for 13 of 15 weeks while volume swings from 1,740 to 9,536 lb, so the second series is a horizontal line and four distribution bars would be one tall bar at Good, one at Easy and two invisible, forever. The real problem underneath both is a rating-scale collapse — 748 of 876 sets rated 4 or 5. Plotting a constant decorates that rather than diagnosing it, and effort_band plus effort_legend already ship.
Per-muscle mechanical fatigue with a 36 h half-life
Already refused as OG2-C1 on measurement, 2 September. Zero of 286 re-training intervals arrive above 50% residual; 49 of 88 exercises have one session so no curve fits; and 162 of 165 weighted slots carry a single distinct weight, so the (load/1RM)^1.5 intensity term collapses to a constant.
Retained strength, or an "expected current 1RM"
Refused twice now. The median re-training gap is 6 days, so 230 of 242 intervals never leave the 14-day plateau, and STRENGTH_FLOOR = 0.5 reports half your best forever — a number with no evidence presented as confidently as a measured one. Fights projection.py's refuse-rather-than-guess rule.
A self-calibrating noise band from the user's own spread
Only 4 of the last 26 weeks have more than one weigh-in, with spreads of 2.60, 0.21, 1.03 and 2.48 kg. A derived band lands around 1.6–2.5 kg — looser than the hard-coded 1.0 — which would newly classify the exact 5 lb regression GOAL-STATE was built to expose as at_start. Any honest minimum-points refusal would fire every time, making the derived path dead code wrapped around the constant it replaced.
Stall-count deload, and the bodyweight add-a-set rung
Both already measured and refused: the maximum consecutive shortfall across 143 exercises with history is 1, and both missing bodyweight rungs would be branches that cannot fire. Note for whoever changes the prefill: openGym's miss predicate is rep-based, and myvitals' prefill makes a rep-miss streak unobservable by construction.

Refuted on structure

Take the superset rest as the maximum over members
pair_supersets only ever pairs isolation exercises — compounds always stand alone — and production confirms it: all 228 superset slots are isolation lifts at 120 s, while the 1,338 non-superset slots span 15–240 s. At goal=hypertrophy and age 48, isolation rest is 60 + 15 = 75 s, so the flat 120 is longer than every member asks. Adopting openGym's rule would cut this user's superset rest by 45 s, reversing a deliberate May 2026 bump. The genuinely unhandled combination — strength, 60+, isolation superset — is unreachable, and is a two-line floor if it ever is.
Cascade a weight edit forward through the session
TD-6 already ships it, server-side and one set later: PlannedSetOut's prefill tiers on the most recently logged set of this exercise in this session, warm-ups tiered separately, and logSet reloads after every set. The claimed correctness tail cannot occur, and re-implementing it in Vue and Compose recreates exactly the divergence TD-6 removed.
Smooth decay on the volume feeding split selection
The sketch's own honest minimum — weighted_sets for score_focus, plain sets for the audit card — is a second number under a different rule sitting one field away in the same response, which is precisely what volume_status was extracted (OG2-C2) to prevent. The consumer is a once-daily pick whose recency half already moves continuously, so a 23:59-versus-00:01 difference is an oddity nobody observes.
Derive PROG-1's state from the log · Conditional writes with a revision
Same root: PROG-1 is enabled: false with an empty lift list, _advance_program_on_complete returns at its second line and has never run, and the only writer that can lose an update to user_equipment.payload is that disabled one. The sketch's own "reset to, with an effective-from date" is stored progression state, plus an O(history) replay in the plan-generation hot path. The house answer to the lost-write shape is already recorded and lighter: add new preferences as scoped merging endpoints. If PROG-1 ships, the fix is a scoped /workout/strength/program-state the config PUT cannot reach.
Stale-suggestion machinery — planHash, applicable, stale_reason
The id-invalidity half is better handled by shortlist #9, which never renders the bad card at all. What remains requires expanding a collapse-by-default Coach card, loading the nudge, navigating away, modifying that exact exercise, returning and tapping Accept — in one session, on one surface, for one user. The worthwhile subset is the one-line swapError, folded into #9. openGym's version also mirrors a plan hash on the client, which the architecture rule forbids.
Verify the APK sha256 before install
Presented as integrity but conceded not to be the boundary. DownloadManager validates against Content-Length and reports STATUS_FAILED on a short download, and Android's installer verifies the v2/v3 signature over the whole file, so any corruption already fails closed. What is bought is a friendlier message on a path that must fail twice first. Keep the 100 KB size floor as a one-if nit.
A randomised history-edit probe
Its headline invariant already ships in a stronger form: backend/tests/test_progression_set_types.py is an AST guard over the thirteen query sites, which catches a new reader that forgets the constant — something a probe cannot do. Production also has zero warm-up and zero drop rows.
A bundle-size guard in CI
openGym ships to strangers on unknown connections; this dashboard is served over LAN to one person from a container on the same subnet, and ECharts dominates the bundle regardless. Revisit only if the dashboard is exposed over Tailscale on cellular and felt slow.
